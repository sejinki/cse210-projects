Title:
Plant Collection Tool

Description / Overview:
This program is for those who are in the plant hobby and want to keep track of all the plants that they have with their name, type of plant, and also to help them keep track of when to water them as well as all the plants that they have killed or will kill. It tracks the health of the plants based on when the user waters their plants and for some plants if they over water them.

Usage:
The user can add a plant and select the species and then the specific name of the plant. They can display all of the plants that they have added. They can also view all of the plants that needs care or upcoming care so they dont kill the plant. They can also view all of the plants that they have killed. They can also display all the plants so they can choose which plants to water.
